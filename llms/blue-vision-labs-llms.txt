## What the platform was

Blue Vision Labs crowdsourced highly detailed 3D maps of entire cities using images
captured from car-mounted smartphone cameras, achieving centimetre-level positioning
without GPS or traditional maps. Developers used an SDK to perform visual positioning
against that map and to place and retrieve persistent AR content.

Documented capability areas (from the archived product site):

- Navigation — AR wayfinding with virtual instructions anchored in the street
- Social — shared experiences where every participant sees the same content
- Gaming — collaborative AR games with many simultaneous users
- Exploration — persistent digital annotations on physical places
- Transportation — live AR location sharing to find a ride on a busy street
- Retail — product and promotion highlighting in-store
